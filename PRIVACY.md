# RoFilter Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

RoFilter is designed to work without collecting personal data.

## Data RoFilter uses

RoFilter stores the filter settings you choose: minimum rating, minimum active-player count, blocked title words, and whether sponsored results should be hidden. These settings are saved through Chrome's sync storage so they may follow the Chrome profile you are signed into.

## Data Apeira Studios receives

Apeira Studios does not receive, store, sell, or share your filter settings, browsing history, Roblox activity, account information, or usage data. RoFilter has no analytics, advertising SDK, or remote code.

## Website access

RoFilter runs on Roblox pages only to read the game-card information visible in the page and hide cards that do not match your settings. This processing happens inside your browser.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact channel listed at [apeirastudios.me](https://apeirastudios.me/).

RoFilter is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.
